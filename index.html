    J Hills, D.E.M.I, Scarfo Da Plug and isaac! each have their hit ISRC attached to an unrelated song. Each package pairs a dispute of the wrong link with a registration of the artist's own work.

    On most of the partly claimed works, one writer has no IPI and no publisher. Identify who that writer is — often the artist — before proposing to complete the claim.

    Where Deezer mixed same-name artists (Steezy, Swank, OCD, Ever, KAZEY J, Adot), confirm the catalog and the hit ISRC belong to the right person first.
